Built for correction, not blind automation

You stay in control of the numbers.

Receipt OCR is never perfect. ReceiptClaim makes every parsed line editable, preserves the original text for review, and refuses to call a settlement complete unless the categories reconcile.

Current validation: all 20 receipt totals in the ICDAR 2019 SROIE sample were parsed exactly after line text was available. Camera OCR accuracy still depends on focus, lighting, angle, and receipt condition.

Local-first privacy

Your receipt is not our business.

Receipt photos are processed on your device. Drafts stay on that device. ReceiptClaim has no account system, analytics, advertising SDK, or cloud receipt upload.
