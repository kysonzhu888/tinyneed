What Hotspot Guard does

Hotspot Guard is a macOS menu bar app that measures how much data each app uses over a metered connection, warns you when one crosses a limit you set, and can step in automatically if you ask it to. It reports what macOS reports. Treat its figures as a close guide, not as your carrier's meter: your carrier's numbers are the ones you are billed on, and only they are authoritative for any dispute with your carrier.

Automatic action is off by default. When you turn it on, you are asking the app to pause, stop, or freeze processes on your Mac. Software that is interrupted can lose unsaved work, and the app cannot know what a given process was in the middle of. Choose the level of response you are comfortable with, and do not enable automatic action for work you cannot afford to have interrupted.
